MASTER'S THESIS IAV / FAU · 2026

Modular High-Voltage Energy Management

Supervisory control for deciding how limited electrical power is shared between charging, the traction battery, auxiliary consumers and safety-related actions.

CONTEXT

During stationary charging, grid power may have to supply both the traction battery and active vehicle loads. When that power is limited, not every request can be satisfied simultaneously.

WHAT I DEVELOPED

01 Charging supervision

Rule-based allocation of external grid power between the battery, HV auxiliaries and LV auxiliary demand.

02 SoC-aware battery protection

Battery SoC and power limits determine whether support is permitted or further discharge must be prevented.

03 Load prioritization & shedding

Available HV auxiliary power is distributed between battery thermal and comfort demand according to a defined priority order.

04 HV safety override

Fault inputs can request a safe state, HV shutdown and DC-link discharge, overriding normal energy-management behavior.

RESULTS & FINDINGS

01

Battery protection changes what the rest of the vehicle can receive.

Low initial SoC combined with limited grid power shifts operation toward Low-SoC Protection and increases unallocated auxiliary energy.

02

Battery support improves load availability, but consumes stored energy.

At higher initial SoC, Battery Discharge Support can reduce unallocated auxiliary energy while lowering final SoC.

03

Priority rules decide what loses power first.

Battery thermal demand is supplied before comfort demand, so comfort is reduced first when HV auxiliary power is constrained.

04

Safety takes precedence over normal EMS objectives.

The selected fault cases activate the intended safe-state request and discharge command instead of continuing normal power allocation.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

The useful result is the trade-off map between grid power, auxiliary demand, battery SoC and priority rules. Rule-based control is transparent and traceable, which is valuable for functional validation, but it is not predictive or energy-optimal.

Scope

Selected supervisory-function development and simulation-based validation — not complete vehicle validation, an optimality proof or safety certification.

PROJECT THESIS FAU · 2025

Adaptive Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects

A local-model approach for manipulating flexible cable shapes without requiring a globally accurate physical model of the object.

CONTEXT

Flexible cables are nonlinear and their behavior changes with configuration and environmental contact. The project investigated whether a sequence of locally valid deformation models can remain useful as the manipulation progresses.

WHAT I DEVELOPED

01 Local Jacobian model

Estimated the local relationship between gripper motion and resulting cable deformation.

02 Adaptive retraining

Updated the local deformation model as the cable moved away from the region where the previous model was valid.

03 Multi-step planning

Used intermediate configurations to guide the cable through larger or contact-constrained shape changes.

04 Simulation evaluation

Evaluated free-space manipulation, a single-contact case and a multiple-contact case in SOFA using Python.

RESULTS & FINDINGS

01

Local models can work well in contact-free shaping.

The local model converged successfully when the cable stayed within a manageable local deformation region.

02

Retraining becomes important as the configuration changes.

Moving farther from the model's locally valid region reduces accuracy, making online model updates part of the control strategy.

03

Contact-rich manipulation is possible, but needs more structure.

More complex tasks required suitable intermediate configurations and careful tuning rather than relying on one model for the entire motion.

04

Tuning strongly affects stability.

Data-window size, retraining interval and related parameters can materially change the behavior; poor tuning can destabilize or fail a task.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

A single globally valid linear model is generally not sufficient for large, contact-dependent deformations. A more useful question is whether locally valid models can be updated quickly enough to remain useful as the task evolves.

Limitation

The framework requires initial calibration, manually selected intermediate configurations for complex cases and was evaluated in simulation rather than on a physical robot.

BACHELOR TEAM PROJECT VIT · 2022

Bidirectional Portable Charger for Electric Vehicles

A simulated charger architecture that treats the EV battery as both an electrical load and an energy source, enabling bidirectional power transfer.

CONTEXT

Conventional charging is one-way. The project investigated a portable bidirectional architecture supporting grid-to-vehicle, vehicle-to-grid and vehicle-to-vehicle energy transfer through controlled power-electronic stages.

WHAT WE DEVELOPED

01 Bidirectional charging stage

Modeled G2V and V2G power transfer between the AC grid and EV battery.

02 LLC resonant conversion

Used an LLC resonant DC/DC stage for high-frequency isolated battery interfacing.

03 Grid interface

Evaluated single-phase and three-phase bidirectional grid-connected arrangements, including an LCL-filtered interface.

04 V2V transfer

Included a direct DC vehicle-to-vehicle rescue mode using the portable charger architecture.

RESULTS & FINDINGS

01

Bidirectional EV-grid power transfer was demonstrated in simulation.

The simulated interface supported energy transfer in both charging and grid-support directions.

02

The common DC link connected the conversion stages.

The architecture used the DC bus as the central electrical interface between the grid-side and battery-side conversion stages.

03

The controller followed changes in active-power demand.

The report concludes that the developed control performed well dynamically for DC-bus stability and tracking a changed active-power reference.

04

The architecture suggests a wider energy-system role.

The report identifies EV batteries as potential microgrid storage and notes that the common DC bus could later integrate renewable sources; this is a future implication, not a validated project result.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

A bidirectional charger is not only a charger; it becomes a controlled power-flow interface between the battery, other vehicles and the grid.

INDUSTRIAL PROJECT CDCE ROBOTICS · 2020

Online Vision Inspection Poka-Yoke System with Unlimited Storage

Machine-vision inspection integrated with industrial control so that detection, decision and physical accept/reject behavior form one automated process.

CONTEXT

The project was completed for Daewon India Auto Parts through CDCE Robotics and focused on repeatable automated inspection of coil-spring components.

WHAT I WORKED ON

01 Machine-vision inspection

Used visual inspection to evaluate coil-spring components automatically.

02 PLC integration

Connected the inspection decision with the wider industrial control sequence.

03 Poka-Yoke response

Used the inspection result to drive the automated accept/reject behavior instead of leaving vision as an isolated diagnostic step.

04 Inspection storage

The project scope included retained inspection information for traceability, reflected in the original project title.

RESULTS & OUTCOMES

01

The evaluated test cases achieved 100% fault-detection accuracy.

This figure refers to the reported project testing, not a claim about indefinite production-line performance.

02

The useful system was the integration, not only the camera.

Inspection, decision logic and machine response were connected into one repeatable workflow.

03

Poka-Yoke shifts the goal from finding faults to preventing incorrect flow.

A failed inspection can directly trigger a machine response rather than relying on a later manual decision.

04

Stored inspection information supports traceability.

Retaining inspection records makes the system useful beyond the instantaneous pass/fail decision.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

The camera is only one component. A practical inspection system requires sensing, decision logic, industrial control and physical action to work together.

Scope

The reported detection figure is limited to project testing/evaluated cases and should not be interpreted as a universal production-performance guarantee.
