HOW I THINK ABOUT ENGINEERING

Software is only half the system.

The problems I enjoy most are not purely software problems and not purely hardware problems.

They involve dynamics, measurements, limitations, uncertainty and failure modes — and then ask software to make sensible decisions inside those constraints.

That is the common thread between an HV energy manager, a robotic manipulation controller and an automated inspection system.
