THE TOOLBOX IS NOT THE POINT

Pick the model. Write the logic. Test the behavior.

MATLAB, Python, Simulink, SOFA or a PLC are different ways of interacting with a system.

The transferable skill is understanding what the system should do, representing it at the right level of abstraction and checking whether the implementation actually produces that behavior.
